# SpaceWar Cats

![screenshot from game](https://i.ibb.co/0pcbFMPn/0luq-Km.png)

## About
After centuries, Earth is entering an era of world peace. Never before has there been such rapid progress in so many years! But something has taken us by surprise: cats, it turns out, were invaders from millennia past who waited for us to develop extremely advanced technologies so they could invade and take everything for themselves! Now it’s up to you, Captain Gomes Manuel, to stop this feline invasion and restore world peace!

---

## Mechanics
This is a shoot 'em up game in which the player controls a spaceship that must defend Earth from an invasion of cats.

The player uses the mouse, aiming the cursor at the enemies to try to hit them, and the small spaceship follows the cursor’s vertical movement. The game is divided into waves; at the end of each one, you can purchase an upgrade for the spaceship or for Earth. Next to the planet, at the top of the screen, there is a health bar that decreases every time a cat hits it.

Left mouse button/Space bar - Shoot

ESC/P - Open game menu
